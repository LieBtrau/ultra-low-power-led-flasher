After Action Report
---
2026-08-30

The idea was to use the light inside of a translucent night light in the shape of a bear.  The light output at 1.5 µA will be too low to notice, even when it's fully dark.

The LED driving current must be increased to make it useful.  By how much?  This testing should have been done at the beginning of the project, using the old prototypes.  Now, considerable effort has already been spent in the design of the schematic.

When the needed LED current is too high to keep it continuously on, some way will be needed to turn it on/off automatically.  That would require a light sensor and a controlling element (MCU).  And so an originally simple projects starts draining my spare time...
